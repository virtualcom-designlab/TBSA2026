## TBSA 2026 Invited Talk

- Presenter: Dr. [Yu Nishitsutsumi](https://cinet.jp/english/people/20220609/)
- Affiliation: Center for Information and Neural Networks (CiNet), Advanced ICT Research Institute, National Institute of Information and Communications Technology (NICT)

- Title of Presentation: 
Technology Acceptance and Autonomy: How Should We Understand Technologies That Transform the Mind?

- Presentation Abstract:
How have new technologies been accepted in society, and under what conditions has such acceptance taken place? This presentation reviews philosophical and ethical debates concerning technology acceptance and autonomy, examining how technologies have shaped human choice and self-understanding. It particularly focuses on emerging technologies that have the potential to transform core aspects of the mind itself, including judgment and decision-making, emotion, and motivation. By doing so, the presentation aims to offer a preliminary conceptual clarification of what is distinctive about these technologies and how they raise issues that differ from those addressed in conventional theories of technology acceptance. (98 words)
